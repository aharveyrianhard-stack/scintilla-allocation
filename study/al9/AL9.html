WHAT COULD BE WRONG

  • Three dials are proposals, not measurements. Conviction at one dollar in four is your figure. That it rises by 5 points of share as the market washes out, and that the breakout band is 5 points of the account, are baselines chosen to be small; nothing in history was tested to set them.
  • The held part of 70% means the number only runs from 70% to 100%. That is the shape shown to you this afternoon (core 45, conviction 25, tactical 30). At the six days you named it moved between 77% and 100%.
  • The history is today's rule looked back on. It says what the tool would say on those prices, not what an older rule said at the time. The rule's test on years it had never seen is in the deployment study.
  • Today's path is rebuilt, not stored. It is worked out again from the market's five-minute prices on every read, so it is the same on any device; an earlier day shows its closing number only.
  • The account is typed, not read from the broker. Cash and share counts are inputs; the baseline is the broker's reading of 7 October at 13:05.
  • Sizes hold the account's value where it is. A buy further down or a trim further up is sized on today's account value.
  • TSMC and Oracle have no reviewed chart lines stored, so their levels are their averages, their usual cool-off and their 60-session high. Oracle is under all four of its averages and shows no buy level.

WHAT WAS NOT DONE

  • The rest of the page is not joined to the number. THE BRIEF and the sector steps still size from the earlier five-step table (50% that afternoon), and step 3b still gives Micron two fifths of that. A note under THE BRIEF and under step 2 says so.
  • Nothing is stored. No table was written or proposed; the five-minute path needs none.
  • Orders are not sent anywhere. The levels and sizes are on the screen only.
  • Not on the live tool. This is a branch; it goes live only on your word.
