TWO THINGS FOUND IN THOSE LINES, AND PUT RIGHT

  • The VIX would have been said twice. The day the VIX closes at 20 or more (or touches it), the live first screen would have shown its line — "the VIX at 21.3: over your 20, +10" — and then a second, false one: "+10 from the parts you switched on". The number itself was right; the words counted the VIX twice. It has not shown yet because the VIX has stayed under 20 since the dashboard went live. Fixed, and tested on a market where the VIX adds.
  • "−0.0%". At tonight's close the credit line read "credit −0.0% over ten sessions". A move that rounds to nothing now carries no sign: "credit 0.0% over ten sessions, −11".

WHAT COULD BE WRONG

  • The whole numbers can be one off. Each caption rounds its own figure, so on some days the three you can add up read one more or one less than the total beside them. At midday the lines said +2, −12 and "40 → 20"; with the typical day's 49 now on the screen, that would have read 49 + 2 − 12 = 39 beside a 40. The bars are drawn at the exact values, and resting the pointer on a row shows its sum to one decimal, but on the Apple TV there is no pointer. Tonight they happen to add (49 + 2 − 11 = 40).
  • A green bar captioned "a little stretched". The word comes from the RSI's level (60 and over), the colour from what the part does — and the RSI part still adds a little until about 62.5. In four lines of text that passed unnoticed; beside a green bar it looks like a contradiction. It is the first decision below.
  • Small parts are small. On a phone the scale is 292 px for 100 points, so a +2 is 6 px wide. It is never thinner than 2 px and a part that adds nothing is a white mark, but it is not a big bar.
  • The rows run across, not side by side. The brief said a waterfall "left to right"; I drew the scale left to right with one row per step, because in a picture this small a +2 would be about 1 px tall as a column. If you meant columns, say so.
  • At +5% the bracket row reads "the swing is halved (0 → 0)". True — cash is still raised there and half of nothing is nothing — but it is a line that says little. It is DB1's wording and I left it.
  • The card is taller. About 95 px more at 1680 (about 115 with seven rows). At 1680 × 1050 everything down to the Reset line is still on the first screen; on a shorter screen the chips move down by that much.
  • The pictures were taken with New York closed (22:06 on a Friday). The live path — a read every minute — was tested by asking the page for a new read, not by watching a session.
  • If the engine ever counts a part the lines do not name, the picture draws the difference as a row called "other parts" rather than leaving it out. That row has only been seen in a test.

  • Not pushed, not deployed. The coordinator merges to main.

  • The deployed page was not pictured — these pictures are the branch served locally the way Vercel serves it, on the live chart API.

DECISIONS FOR ALAN

  1. "A little stretched" on a bar that adds. Recommendation: start that word where the RSI part turns from adding to taking away (about 62.5), so a green bar is never captioned "stretched". One threshold, one line of code.
  2. Whole numbers or one decimal in the captions. Recommendation: keep whole numbers — they are what the long version says and what you would say out loud — and accept that some days they are one off against the total. If that bothers you the first time you see it, one decimal is a one-line change.
  3. Rows or columns. Recommendation: keep the rows. They read at phone width without a second design, and the small parts stay visible.
PAGE SPECS

Where it lives. study/db2/how.mjs draws the picture (howSteps: the sum; howHtml: the drawing; HOW_CSS: its look). study/db1/dashboard.mjs calls it in the fourth card and makes the lines (summaryLines) — now also for a what-if move. study/ds1/live.mjs hands each what-if move its own reading whole (r) and the day its raise-cash state began (since); nothing new is fetched. No library; the colours are the dashboard's own (the tool's green and red for up and down, its text white, its dim grey).

The sum. A typical day (the model's own, 49.08) + the RSI part + the credit part as counted (after version 2's softening and version 3's treasury rule) + the VIX's add by your 20 / 23 rule + any part you switched on = the level before cash; kept inside 0–100; halved while cash is raised = the market reading. The number = 70 + 30 × the market reading ÷ 100, on the dials under ASSUMPTIONS. The picture does this sum itself and compares it with the engine's reading; the difference is 0 to six decimals on every case tested.

Why the VIX was said twice. Version 3 passes the VIX's add to version 2 through the same slot as the parts the viewer switches on. DB1's line read that slot whole. Now only what is left after the VIX's own add is called "the parts you switched on".

Why every class starts with hw-. The first drawing gave one bar the plain class "under"; the dashboard's sheet has a rule of that name (the line under the big number) and the bar came out 2 px tall. It was caught by looking at the picture. A test now holds that no class in the picture is a plain name.

Pictures. scripts/db2-shots.mjs, headless, one page at a time, every request that is not a read blocked (none was attempted), drawn at twice the pixels so the thin marks can be examined: 18 pictures in study/db2/pictures, with what was on the screen for each in study/db2/data/db2-shots.json.
