WHAT COULD BE WRONG

  • Version 3 is bolder exactly when it hurts most to be wrong. Both new rules add in a fall. Every panic in the nine unseen years turned within months. In 2004–2009 version 3 ended at 0.53×, version 2 at 0.55×, buying and holding at 0.66×.
  • 20 candidates were tried. 2 passed both bars alone. With that many tries one can pass by luck; the VIX steps were ahead of version 2 every one of the four ways the test was run, and at every level I moved them to, which is why I trust them more than a single pass.
  • The treasury rule is in on judgement, not on the bar. It does not move a scored date by a point. It was added after the first run, when the first form turned out to cut the number on flight-to-safety days. It has its own switch on the pane.
  • The VIX's high. The study used the Hub's daily bar for the VIX. If TradingView's bar has a different high, a touch can count on one and not the other.
  • "Both hands above 23" is 6 points of the account here, not all of it. Bigger steps held more and earned no more, so they failed on timing. If you mean more than that, it is a number to set with you, with its cost beside it.
  • The steps' sizes are round numbers I fixed before the run, not measured ones. Measured, they say "all in above 20", which failed.
  • No costs, taxes or payouts in the replay, as in the first two rounds.

Where each number comes from. Every figure is read from the study's own files when the page is built: study/ds3/data/ds3.json (the candidates, the dates, the replay), ds3-live.json (version 3's rules), ds3-pane-proof.json (the pane) and ds3-morning.json (this morning's two reads). The study is scripts/ds3-build.mjs; version 3's sum is study/ds3/number.mjs.

The number. % invested = 70 held + 30 tactical × the market reading ÷ 100. The market reading = a typical day (49.1) + the RSI part + the credit part + what the VIX adds, held to 0–100, halved while cash is raised. A point of market reading is 0.30 of a point of the account.

The test. The first study's replay, through the same code the last round used. Before scoring anything the build checks that it gives the first study's stored replay (3.54×, −30.0%, 85.0%) and that its version 2 gives the last round's stored result and dates to the last digit. Fitted on 2,160 days, replayed on 2,182. The share set at a close earns the next session's blend of SPY and QQQ; cash earns the 3-month bill.

The VIX. The Hub's daily bar: its close (from the Hub's own table where it has one) and its high. A year's place = the share of the last 252 closes under it.
